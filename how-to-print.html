How to print from this computer

You only need to find the library printer once. After that, it stays selected until you log out.

  1. Open printing

    Pick one way:

    Way 1Click ⋮ at the top right, then click Print
    OR
    Way 2Press Ctrl + P

  2. Click “See more”

    Next to Destination, open the list and choose See more.

    Destination
    Save as PDF
    See more…

  3. Choose Smart Alec

    Pick Smart Alec (Library Printing), then click Print.

    Select a destination
    Save as PDF
    Smart Alec (Library Printing)

  4. Log in

    Use your library card and PIN, or choose Guest User. Your document sends on its own.

    Card Number / Email
    PIN
    Log in
    Guest User

Printing Tips

Print only some pagesNext to Pages, choose Custom. Type the pages you want, like 3-4.

Turn the page sidewaysNext to Layout, choose Landscape. This works well for wide pages and pictures.

Pick up your printing at any library Print Release Station.

You can close this tab when you’re ready.
